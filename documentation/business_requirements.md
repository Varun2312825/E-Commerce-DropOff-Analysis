BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS

## Context

VoltEdge is a mid-sized online retailer focused on computers and electronics. Site traffic is healthy, but revenue is not growing in step with visits. Leadership suspects that shoppers engage with products and then leave before buying.

## Problem

Most sessions view products without purchasing. The business cannot see where in the journey shoppers drop out, or which parts of the catalogue lose the most value.

## Stakeholders and their questions(Hypothetical)

| Stakeholder | Question | Answered on page |
|---|---|---|
| Head of E-commerce | Where do we lose the most conversions? | Overview, Funnel |
| Merchandising / category managers | Which categories and brands leak value? | Product, Segments |
| Product / UX | Is a specific funnel stage broken? | Funnel, Segments |
| Marketing | Are we driving views to products that convert, and when? | Product, Time |
| Finance | How much revenue is at stake? | Overview, Time (scenario) |

## Requirements

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| BR1 | where do users abandon the funnel |
| BR2 | what are under-performing categories, brands and price bands |
| BR3 | conversion patterns by hour of day and day of week |
| BR4 | Quantifying revenue and the value at stake |
| BR5 | actionable recommendations |

In scope: the view → cart → purchase funnel, with category, brand, price and time dimensions.
Out of scope: device, marketing channel, geography and checkout-form steps (no data).
Success criteria: every question below is answered with a validated number, prioritised recommendations, each with a KPI to monitor.
